    // Frequently asked questions

    Frequently asked questions about this service

    Which platforms do you work with?

    For hardware, Arduino, ESP32 and Raspberry Pi. For software, JavaScript, Python, Firebase or Supabase, depending on the project.

    How long does an IoT project take?

    As a reference, a prototype or proof of concept takes 1–2 weeks, a complete working product 3–6 weeks and a home or business automation 2–5 weeks.

    Do you work for industry and agriculture, or only homes?

    All three: sensing, automation and remote monitoring systems are designed for homes, industry and agriculture.

    How much does an electronics or IoT project cost?

    Every project is quoted individually and for free: tell us what you want to measure or automate and you get price and timeline in under 24 hours, with no commitment.

    What happens after delivery?

    You get documentation and follow-up support: we answer questions, fine-tune details and leave everything working. The relationship doesn't end at delivery.
