// Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions about this service

What file do I need for 3D printing?

Ideally an STL file. A drawing or just the idea also works: in that case we design the part in CAD before manufacturing it.

Do you ship outside Medellín?

Yes. Physical parts (3D printing, laser and CNC) are shipped by courier anywhere in Colombia.

How much does it cost to 3D print a part?

It depends on the volume, material, infill and number of parts. The site's estimator gives you an instant estimate of time, weight and material, and the final quote arrives free in under 24 hours, with no commitment.

Which materials do you work with?

For FDM printing: PLA, PETG, ABS and TPU. For detail printing, standard resin. For laser cutting, MDF, acrylic and wood; for CNC, wood, aluminum, steel or plastic.

Can I check my model before sending it?

Yes. This site's 3D viewer opens your STL right in the browser, without uploading it to any server, so you can rotate and inspect it, and passes it to the estimator to see how long it would take to print.
