    // Frequently asked questions

    Frequently asked questions about this service

    How long does it take to build a web application?

    As a reference, a custom web application takes 4–10 weeks, a landing page 1–2 weeks and a process automation 2–5 weeks. We work in stages, so you see real progress from the first days.

    Which technologies do you use?

    JavaScript, Next.js, Python, FastAPI, Supabase and Firebase, plus AI models like Gemini when the project needs them.

    Can you build my own SaaS product?

    Yes. TaxiYa and IncubApp are CDH Maker SaaS products, and YAM (A Tiempo Logística) is a last-mile SaaS platform for e-commerce in Medellín. All three can be seen live.

    Do you work with companies outside Medellín or Colombia?

    Yes. Software is delivered in the cloud anywhere in the world, and we work in Spanish and English.

    How is development paid?

    By milestones: the project moves forward in stages with partial deliveries, and payment is split across those stages. The quote is free and arrives in under 24 hours, with no fine print.
