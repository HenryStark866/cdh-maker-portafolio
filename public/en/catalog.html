    // Frequently asked questions

    Questions about pricing and consulting

    I don't know what my business needs. Where do I start?

    With the free 30-minute orientation call: you tell us how you work today and what hurts, and we tell you what kind of solution fits, whether it's worth it and roughly what it would cost. If more detail is needed, the express digital diagnosis costs USD 90 (290,000 COP) and is credited if you hire the project.

    What is CDH Maker's consulting and who is it for?

    It is an engineer helping you decide before you spend: a business diagnosis, a technology roadmap (what to build, what to buy and in which order) and monthly fractional-CTO advisory. It is for small businesses, merchants and founders in Colombia and worldwide, in Spanish or English.

    How much does a website, online store or custom app cost?

    As a reference: a landing page from USD 470, an online store from USD 875 and a custom web app or SaaS from USD 1,900. Prices are indicative; the final price depends on scope and the quote is free.

    How much does an AI assistant for WhatsApp cost?

    From USD 780 (2,500,000 COP), trained on your business information, connected to WhatsApp Business or your website, with handoff to a person when needed.

    Do prices include taxes?

    No: prices are before taxes and indicative. The formal quote details taxes, stages and milestone payments.

    What is YAM?

    YAM is CDH Maker's own platform for merchants to sell online with last-mile delivery and cash on delivery in Medellín. It charges 2.5% of paid sales, billed monthly, with no fixed monthly fee.
