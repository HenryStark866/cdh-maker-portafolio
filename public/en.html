    // Frequently asked questions

    What everyone wants to know

    Clear answers before you start.

    How much does a project cost?

    Every project is unique, so quotes are personalized and free: describe your idea and receive price and timing in under 24 hours, no strings attached.

    How do work and payment happen?

    We work in stages with partial deliveries: you see real progress from day one and payment is split by milestones. No surprises, no fine print.

    Do you serve clients outside Medellín?

    Yes. Software is delivered in the cloud anywhere in the world, and physical parts (3D, laser, CNC) ship by courier across Colombia.

    What happens after delivery?

    You get documentation and follow-up support: we answer questions and fine-tune details until everything runs smoothly.

    How long does a project take?

    It depends on the scope. As a reference: a landing page takes 1 to 2 weeks, a custom web application 4 to 10, a process automation 2 to 5, an electronics or IoT prototype 1 to 2 weeks, and a CAD design 2 to 5 days. The estimator on this page gives you an estimate for your case.

    Can you integrate artificial intelligence into my company or product?

    Yes. We build AI assistants, content generation and AI analytics integrated into real systems: for example EvaIA (a local assistant with voice and vision), SIGI (in progress; AI content with human approval) and IncubApp (industrial operations with AI).

    What technologies do you use?

    Software: JavaScript, Next.js, Python, FastAPI, Supabase, Firebase and AI models such as Gemini. Hardware: Arduino, ESP32 and Raspberry Pi. Fabrication: FDM and resin 3D printing, laser cutting, CNC and 2D/3D CAD design.

    Do you offer made-to-order 3D printing, laser cutting or CNC?

    Yes, in Medellín with shipping anywhere in Colombia. You can send us your STL file, your drawing or just the idea for us to design, and check your model first in this page's 3D viewer to estimate the printing time.

    Who is behind CDH Maker?

    CDH Maker is the brand of Ing. Henry Taborda, an engineer and maker in Medellín, Colombia. You talk directly with the person who designs, builds and delivers your project, in Spanish or English.

    I don't know what my business needs. Where do I start?

    With a free 30-minute orientation call: you tell us how you work today and what costs you time or money, and we tell you what kind of solution fits and roughly what it would cost. If more detail is needed, the express digital diagnosis costs USD 90 and is credited if you hire the project. Prices in the <a href="/en/catalog">catalog</a>.
